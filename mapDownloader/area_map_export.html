Area Map Export

Pick an area on the map (draw a box, or enter coordinates), pull the OpenStreetMap data for it, and export a line-map SVG with the exact bounds labeled on it. This queries OSM's public Overpass API directly from your browser, so keep areas small (a few blocks to a neighborhood) — large areas will be slow or get rejected.
